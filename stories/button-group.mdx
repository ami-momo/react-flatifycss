# Button group

<p className="component-doc-intro">Put related buttons next to each other and they stick together, nice for toolbars and filters.</p>
<div className="funky-spacer" />

## Default

Wrap your `<Button />`s with `<ButtonGroup />`.

<Canvas of={ButtonGroupStories.Default} />

## Vertical

Add the `vertical` prop to stack them.

<Canvas of={ButtonGroupStories.Vertical} />

## Bordered

The group does not change your buttons, so to have borders add `bordered` to each `<Button />`.

<Canvas of={ButtonGroupStories.Bordered} />
