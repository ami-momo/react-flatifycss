## Credit

Give credit to the photographer with `creditName`, add `creditLink` to make it a link and `creditTheme` to change its color.

<Canvas of={FigureStories.CreditName} />

<Canvas of={FigureStories.CreditTheme} />

## Centered and fill

`centered` puts the figure in the middle of its parent, `fill` makes it as wide as its parent.
